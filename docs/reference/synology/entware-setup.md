⚙️ **Entware** enhances your Synology NAS by enabling the installation of additional software packages like `make`, `gcc`, `python3`, and more. This is perfect for setting up environments such as Homebridge for home automation and other advanced tools! 🚀

## 🛠️ Installation Steps

1. **Follow the Entware Installation Guide:**
   👉 Visit the [Entware Installation Guide for Synology NAS](https://github.com/Entware/Entware/wiki/Install-on-Synology-NAS) and follow the steps specific to your NAS model and DSM version.
