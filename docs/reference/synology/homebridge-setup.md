🏠 **Homebridge** is a lightweight Node.js server that emulates the iOS HomeKit API, allowing you to integrate a variety of smart home devices not officially supported by Apple. By installing Homebridge on your Synology NAS, you can centralize your home automation and manage your devices with ease! 🔧✨

## 🚀 Installation Steps

1. **Follow the Homebridge Installation Guide:**
   👉 Head over to the [Homebridge Installation Guide for Synology DSM](https://github.com/homebridge/homebridge/wiki/Install-Homebridge-on-Synology-DSM) and follow the steps specific to your Synology NAS model and DSM version.
